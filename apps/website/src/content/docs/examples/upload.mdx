## What it shows

The two ways bytes reach vola, chosen by size. A file under 1 MiB is one multipart `POST`. A file above it goes through the resumable protocol in 256 KiB chunks, with a progress bar. The site sets those numbers so the resumable path has room under the demo's cap. `@vola/react`'s own threshold is 8 MiB.

Files land in the `uploads` folder. The demo's limits are listed on the page: 5 MiB per file, a list of allowed types, a purge after 24 hours and 60 writes per address per ten minutes. [Uploads and processing](/concepts/uploads-and-processing/) explains what happens after the bytes arrive.

## What to try

- Drop a small image, then follow the link to Browse to see it.
- Upload a file of 2 to 5 MiB, cancel it halfway, and resume. The server keeps the offset and the browser keeps a fingerprint, so a page reload resumes too.
- Upload a file over 5 MiB (`413`) or of a type not on the list (`415`).

## Routes

| Route | What for |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /vola/examples/write/files/:type/:id` | The multipart upload, replayed against `POST /vola/files/:type/:id` |
| `POST /vola/examples/write/uploads` | Creating a resumable upload |
| `HEAD`, `PATCH`, `DELETE` on `/vola/examples/write/uploads/:id` | Checking, sending and cancelling it |

## `@vola/react` pieces

`useVolaUpload` holds the upload state, `VolaUploadButton` picks the files, and `VolaUploadList` draws progress, cancel and resume. The drop zone around them is the page's own.
