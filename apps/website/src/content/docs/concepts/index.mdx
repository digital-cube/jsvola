vola is a file service that runs beside your application. Your application hands it files and gets back ids, downloads with `Range`, thumbnails, OCR text, share links, folders and versions, separated per tenant and per entity in your own data model.

These pages explain how that works and why. For the exact routes, headers and error codes, go to the [Reference](/reference/). To run something first, go to [Get started](/get-started/).

## Storage and identity

[Files and storage](/concepts/files-and-storage/) covers the model everything else builds on.

- How a blob is stored once under its SHA-256, and when it leaves the disk.
- The instance model: every file belongs to a tenant and to an entity of your application.
- Folders, slugs and versions.
- Tenants, API keys and scopes.

Read it first if you are new to vola.

## What happens to an upload

[Uploads and processing](/concepts/uploads-and-processing/) follows the bytes after they arrive.

- The two steps every upload ends in.
- Resumable uploads over tus.
- The worker and its queue.
- Thumbnails and the `202` rules every client has to follow.
- OCR and full-text search.

## Encryption at rest

[Encryption](/concepts/encryption/) describes the four modes (`tenant`, `keyring`, `caller` and `client`), who holds which key, what each mode costs you and what a stolen dump reveals. The byte-level format is in the [crypto reference](/reference/crypto/).

## Live lists

[Real-time](/concepts/real-time/) explains the `/udc/subscribe` WebSocket: what it carries, who publishes the events and how a list on screen stays current without polling.

## The public demo

[The examples site](/concepts/examples-site/) explains how a public site can let anonymous visitors upload, share and set a PIN: a read-only key in the browser, a write proxy with a policy, a rate limit and a purge. The pages themselves are under [Examples](/examples/).

## Coming from the old service

[Migrating from vola-spp](/concepts/migrating-from-vola-spp/) is for applications that call vola-spp today. It lists what changes on the wire, what stays the same and a checklist per consumer. There is no data migration.
