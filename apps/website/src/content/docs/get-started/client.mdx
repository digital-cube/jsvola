## Two answers to handle

**A thumbnail can answer 202.** That status counts as success, and its body is a grey placeholder, so `vola.files.thumb` tells you which one you got through `status`: `ready`, `generating` or `failed`. Retry `generating` with a backoff, and don't cache it.

**A failed call throws a `VolaError`** carrying the server's `status`, `code` and `message`. A file that needs an unlock it doesn't have throws `VolaLockedError`, and a wrong key or damaged bytes throw `VolaIntegrityError`.

## What else it covers

Resumable uploads that continue after an abort, folders, share links, keyrings and their unlock tokens, statistics and UDC collections. The [package README](https://github.com/digital-cube/jsvola/blob/main/packages/client/README.md) has an example of each.
